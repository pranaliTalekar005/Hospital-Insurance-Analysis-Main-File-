# 🏥 Hospital and Insurance Analysis using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Hospital and Insurance Analysis project is a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze insurance policies, premium revenue, claim management, and customer feedback.

The main objective of this project is to understand insurance business performance, monitor policy renewals, analyze claims, and identify customer complaints to support better business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze total insurance premium revenue.
- Understand policy distribution based on renewal status.
- Track monthly policy sales trends.
- Analyze revenue across different geographical locations.
- Monitor insurance claims and their status.
- Understand customer complaints and feedback.
- Identify areas for improving customer satisfaction and business performance.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Policy Overview
- Policy Distribution by Renewal Status.
- Monthly Policy Sales Trend.
- Geographic Revenue Distribution.
- Premium Revenue by Policy Type.
- Total Premium Amount KPI.
- Policy Type KPI.

### 2. Claim Management
- Analyze insurance claims.
- Monitor claim status.
- Understand claim distribution and trends.
- Identify patterns in claim management.

### 3. Customer Support & Feedback
- Analyze customer complaints.
- Understand customer feedback.
- Identify common customer issues.
- Monitor customer support performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Cleaning and Transformation

## 📈 Key Insights

- Understand the distribution of renewed and non-renewed insurance policies.
- Identify monthly trends in insurance policy sales.
- Compare premium revenue across different policy types.
- Analyze geographical revenue distribution.
- Understand claim management performance.
- Identify customer complaints and areas requiring improvement.

## 💡 Business Benefits

- Helps management make data-driven decisions.
- Supports better insurance policy management.
- Helps identify opportunities to improve policy renewals.
- Improves understanding of claim management.
- Supports better customer service and satisfaction.

## 🚀 Future Scope

This dashboard can be expanded by integrating data from other departments to improve overall organizational productivity, identify business requirements, and support better strategic planning.
